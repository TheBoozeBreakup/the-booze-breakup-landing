THE BOOZE BREAKUP STARTS WITH A DIFFERENT IDEA:

Before deciding what role alcohol should play in your life, understand your current relationship with it.

The Booze Breakup looks at alcohol through the lens of relationships, curiosity, and personal reflection rather than black-and-white labels. It’s not anti-alcohol or prescriptive about quitting. Drawing from personal experience, years of conversations, and stories from people whose Booze Breakups led to different outcomes, the book invites readers to examine their own relationship with alcohol without being told what that relationship should become.
