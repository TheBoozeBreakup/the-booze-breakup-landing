THE BOOZE BREAKUP STARTS WITH A DIFFERENT IDEA:

Before deciding what role alcohol should play in your life, understand your current relationship with it.

The Booze Breakup looks at alcohol through the lens of relationships, curiosity, and personal reflection rather than black-and-white labels. It’s not anti-alcohol or prescriptive about quitting. Drawing from personal experience, years of conversations, and stories from people who took their own breaks from alcohol, the book invites readers to take a closer look at a relationship that often goes unquestioned before deciding whether anything needs to change.
